# Welcome to Medhira Research

Medhira Research is an independent student-led learning and collaboration space for people entering technology from any starting point.

## If you are completely new

1. Learn basic computer skills: files, folders, browsers, downloads, and online safety.
2. Create a GitHub account and learn what a repository and README are.
3. Choose one beginner direction for 30 days instead of trying everything at once.
4. Practise for 20–30 minutes regularly.
5. Record what you learned and what confused you.
6. Ask questions respectfully and share your progress.

## How to ask for help

When asking a question, include:

- What you are trying to do
- What you already tried
- The exact error or confusing part
- A screenshot or small code sample when useful

Avoid sharing passwords, API keys, private documents, or personal information.

## How to contribute

You can contribute by:

- Fixing a typo
- Adding a beginner explanation
- Sharing a reliable learning resource
- Writing a small tutorial
- Helping another learner understand a concept
- Suggesting improvements through a GitHub issue

## Peer, senior, and teacher participation

People may participate as learners, peer guides, senior contributors, or mentors. Anyone listed publicly must explicitly agree to be listed, and no participant is expected to provide guaranteed mentorship, internships, referrals, or placements.

## Community values

Be patient, inclusive, honest, curious, and respectful. Beginners should feel safe asking basic questions.
