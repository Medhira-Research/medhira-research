Who the hell am I?

I’m an Integrated M.Tech CSE–AIML student at SRM University AP. I’m interested in technology, but I’m not interested in pretending I already know everything. I want to understand how things work by building them, breaking them, asking questions and trying again.

Medhira is my big experiment: part learning lab, part community, part public notebook. I don’t have a grand institution hiding behind this website. It starts with me, a laptop, curiosity and a frankly unreasonable number of ideas.

What I’m doing right now

  • Learning Python from the absolute basics, one day at a time.
  • Working towards backend development with FastAPI.
  • Trying to understand deployment, Docker, CI/CD and cloud systems.
  • Building Medhira into a real learning-by-doing environment.
  • Trying to turn learning into actual projects instead of collecting tutorials.
