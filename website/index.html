04 / THE MEDHIRA VIBE

Backchodi is part of the curriculum.

We believe curiosity does not have to be boring. Explore, mess around, experiment, laugh at your bugs, and find out what happens—but keep it respectful.

01

Fuck around. Find out.

Try things beyond your comfort zone. Break small things, learn why they broke, and build again.

02

Chill, but contribute.

Talk in Telugu, Hindi, Bengali, English, or whatever works. Say hi, make friends, share resources, and help each other learn.

03

Friendly chaos. Real respect.

Jokes, banter, and harmless backchodi are welcome among friends. Respect boundaries, consent, and people’s dignity.

04

CGPA may be suffering.

Academic struggles are real, but we do not use them as an excuse to stop learning. We improve together, one practical step at a time.

A niche circle for curious people

Do everything: chill, learn, collaborate, teach each other, ask dumb questions, and grow together. No language or background barrier—just curiosity and mutual respect.
