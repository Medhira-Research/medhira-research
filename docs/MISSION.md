# Mission

## Vision

To build a student-led environment where curiosity becomes reproducible research, useful software, and documented evidence.

## Mission

Medhira Research explores intelligent systems and engineering problems through:

1. Small, well-defined research questions.
2. Reproducible experiments and transparent documentation.
3. Open-source tools, datasets, and research notes whenever ethically and legally possible.
4. Cross-disciplinary collaboration between students and mentors.
5. Responsible communication of results, limitations, and uncertainty.

## Initial Research Themes

- AI and machine learning
- Computer vision and scientific image analysis
- Image forensics and media integrity
- Cloud and backend engineering
- Developer tools for research workflows

## Working Principles

- Evidence before claims
- Reproducibility over hype
- Ethical and privacy-aware experimentation
- Credit based on actual contributions
- Learning through building and peer review
