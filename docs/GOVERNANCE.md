# Governance

## 1. Nature of the Collective

Medhira Research is an independent student-led initiative. It is not a university department, legal entity, placement agency, or funding body.

## 2. Contributions and Authorship

- Credit should reflect meaningful intellectual, technical, experimental, writing, or project-management contributions.
- Contributors should agree on expected roles and credit before major submissions.
- Authorship decisions should be documented and revisited when project roles change.
- Copying, plagiarism, fabricated results, and undisclosed use of external work are not permitted.

## 3. Research Integrity

- Record datasets, preprocessing steps, model versions, parameters, and evaluation methods.
- Report negative results and limitations where relevant.
- Avoid overstating performance or claiming causation without evidence.
- Preserve experiment logs and version-control important changes.

## 4. Data, Privacy, and Safety

- Use data only when its license, consent, and intended use permit the planned work.
- Do not publish personal, confidential, or sensitive data without appropriate authorization.
- Anonymize data when possible and remove unnecessary identifiers.
- Flag security, privacy, or safety concerns before releasing code or results.

## 5. Opportunities

Opportunities shared by Medhira Research should be checked against official sources when possible. Listings are informational only; the collective does not guarantee selection, payment, internships, jobs, publication, or funding.

## 6. Collaboration

Disagreements should be handled respectfully and documented when they affect project scope, credit, access, or publication. Serious concerns may be escalated to the project lead for review.

## 7. Independence and Conflicts

Contributors should disclose relevant conflicts of interest, sponsorships, institutional restrictions, or external obligations that could affect a project.
